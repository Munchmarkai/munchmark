Privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026. Contact: [support email]

Munchmark is a calorie and nutrition tracker that keeps your log on your own phone. There is no account, no advertising and no selling or sharing of your data for advertising.

What stays on your phone

  • Everything you log: foods, meals, recipes, pantry, water, weights, body stats, sports check-ins, settings and small photo thumbnails.
  • It's stored in your browser's storage on this device. We can't see it. Backups are files you export and keep yourself.
  • An optional Gemini key you enter stays on this phone and is never put in backups.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • Food lookups. Words you search, words read from a label and barcode numbers are sent to Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central to find nutrition. Photos are not sent to them.
  • The on-device recognizer. If you download it, its model files come from Hugging Face. Your photos are then analyzed on the phone and not uploaded.
  • Gemini AI (optional). Only after you tap "I'm 18+, use Gemini", the photo, your details or the typed food you ask about are sent through our server on Cloudflare to the Google Gemini API, and the answer comes back to your phone. Our server doesn't keep photos or text. Cloudflare may keep standard request logs, such as IP addresses, for a short time.
    • On the free plan, Google may use what's sent to improve its products, and people may review it. Don't send anything private.
    • On Munchmark Plus (paid), requests use Google's paid tier, where Google doesn't use them to improve its products.
    • If you add your own Gemini key, requests go straight from your phone to Google under your own Google account's terms.
  • Payments (Munchmark Plus). Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy or Google Play. We receive your email and whether your subscription is active, never your card details.
  • Visit counts. If enabled, Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page visits without cookies and without identifying you.

Health information

Body stats, weights and what you eat can be sensitive. They stay on your phone. The only health-related content that ever leaves it is what you choose to send to Gemini, after you agree.

Age

Munchmark isn't meant for children under 13. Gemini features are only for people 18 or older, as Google's terms require; younger people can use everything else, which runs on the phone.

Where you live

The free Gemini plan isn't offered in the EU, the EEA, the UK or Switzerland. The rest of the app works there.

Deleting your data

Delete the app from your home screen and clear this site's data in your browser settings. That removes everything, because nothing is stored on our servers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
